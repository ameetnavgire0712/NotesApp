Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

What types of content can I save?

Tweets, LinkedIn posts, Instagram reels, YouTube shorts, Facebook posts, Reddit threads, screenshots, photos, voice memos, PDFs, Word documents, Excel sheets, PowerPoint files and any webpage.

How does SnapBot search work?

Every snap is parsed, summarized and indexed by AI. When you ask, SnapBot retrieves the most relevant chunks via hybrid search (vector + keyword + rerank) and answers in plain English with citations.

How do Groups work?

Create a private group from the Groups tab in the Android app, pick a photo, invite friends by email. Anyone in the group can share their snaps to it; everyone else gets them in real time with reactions.

Is my data private and secure?

100%. Only you and group members you invite can see your content. We never sell or share your data. Sign-in is via Google OAuth, storage runs on enterprise cloud with automated backups.

Is there a storage limit?

Free includes 100MB across all your content and up to 10 private groups (currently). Pro (₹399/mo) raises storage limits and allows unlimited group creation with priority indexing.
